About this free DP maker

WhatsApp shows profile photos as a circle, but uploads them as a square — so faces get cut off if you don't position carefully. This tool shows you both: the square you'll download and the exact circular crop WhatsApp displays. Drag to frame your face perfectly, zoom in for a headshot or out for a full photo, then download a crisp 640×640 image. Everything runs in your browser; your photo is never uploaded anywhere.
