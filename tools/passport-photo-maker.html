How to make a passport size photo

  1. Upload a clear front-facing photo with a plain background.
  2. Pick 35×45mm (most countries) or 2×2 inch (USA/India visa).
  3. Drag so your face fills most of the frame, eyes level.
  4. Download and print at any photo studio — or print at home at 300 DPI.

About this free passport photo maker

Official passport photos must be 35×45mm in most countries (2×2 inch for US visas), with your face taking up 70–80% of the frame. This tool crops your photo to the exact ratio at print resolution, so it comes out the right physical size at 300 DPI. For best results use a photo with a plain light background, face the camera directly, and keep a neutral expression. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no watermark, no account.
