About this free image compressor

Large photos slow down websites, eat phone storage, and get rejected by upload forms. This compressor re-encodes your image at the quality you choose — 80% looks identical to the original for most photos while cutting size dramatically. The live preview shows you exactly what you're getting before you download. JPG, PNG and WebP are supported; output is optimized JPEG for photos. Everything happens in your browser, so there's no upload wait and your images stay private.
